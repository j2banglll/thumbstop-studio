FAQ

Questions brands ask first.

I don't have any footage. Can you still help?

Yes. Phone videos of the product are enough to start. I can also tell you exactly what to film, shot by shot, so a friend or a UGC creator can record it in 20 minutes.

How is this different from hiring a cheap editor?

A cheap edit cuts clips together. I plan the ad around the hook, the objection, and the offer, and give you variations to test. You're paying for ads that are built to sell, not just to look nice.

How fast can I get my ads?

Most ads are delivered within 48 hours of receiving your footage. Rush delivery is available if you have a launch date.

What if I don't like the edit?

Every package includes two revision rounds. And the first test ad is free, so you see my work on your product before you pay anything.

How do I pay?

50% upfront and 50% on delivery for packages, monthly for Scale. PayPal, Wise, and bank transfer are all fine.
